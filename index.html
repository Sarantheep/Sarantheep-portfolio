Turf Booking App

UI/UX Case Study for Players and Venue Managers

Problem Statement

Players struggle to book sports turfs because of poor real-time availability, unclear pricing, double bookings, and difficult payment methods. Turf owners also struggle with schedule management and customer reach.

The Solution

The app helps users search nearby turfs, check live availability, compare pricing, make secure bookings, and manage schedules easily — all in one intuitive interface.

Design Process

Empathize

User interviews & surveys with 10 participants

Define

Pain points identified & problem framed

Ideate

Feature brainstorming & concept sketches

Prototype

High-fidelity screens in Figma & XD

Testing

Usability tests with target users
