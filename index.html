Turf Booking App

UI/UX Case Study for Players and Venue Managers

Problem

Players struggle to book sports turfs because of poor real-time availability, unclear pricing, double bookings, and difficult payment methods. Turf owners also struggle with schedule management and customer reach.

Solution

The app helps users search nearby turfs, check live availability, compare pricing, make secure bookings, and manage schedules easily from one unified platform.

Design Process

01

Empathize

User interviews and surveys with 10 participants via Google Forms

02

Define

Synthesized pain points and defined core user problems

03

Ideate

Sketched flows, features, and interface concepts

04

Prototype

High-fidelity Figma screens with full user flows

05

Testing

Usability testing and iterative refinement

Target Users

  • Players and sports enthusiasts
  • Turf owners and managers
  • Event organizers and coaches

Key Research Findings

  • Users want real-time slot availability
  • Simple, fast booking flow needed
  • Online payment a priority
  • Reminders, reviews & loyalty rewards
  • Better turf discovery for sports
